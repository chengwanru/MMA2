### 1. The "5-Second" Visual Strategy (Core Mechanics)
*   **The Grid:** 2 Rows (Top: Standard, Bottom: Ours) x 3 Columns (Memories, Context, Models).
*   **The Anchor:** Column 1 (Memories) and Column 3 (Models) share the **exact same X and Y dimensions/alignments** across both rows. 
*   **The Punchline:** Column 2 (Context). In Row 1, a massive red-tinted rectangle pushes into the Models. In Row 2, a tiny rectangle leaves a huge swath of **stark white negative space**, visually instantly communicating "saved compute."
*   **Removal of Clutter:** *Delete the "VS" badge and internal text captions.* Rely on the stark visual contrast and standard (a)/(b) subfigure labeling.
