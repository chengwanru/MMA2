### 1. Canvas & Top-Level Global Structure
*   **Background:** Clean white (#FFFFFF).
*   **Typography:** Sans-serif (e.g., Arial or Helvetica), minimum 14pt equivalent, bold for component titles, regular italics for math equations.
*   **Global Top Header:** Draw a thin, elegant horizontal band across the top of the entire figure.
    *   *Left side:* "Egocentric Video Stream + User Query"
    *   *Middle/Right side:* Flows into "Output Response Tokens".
*   **Panel Enclosures:** Divide the canvas into two distinct halves with soft, rounded rectangle backgrounds (8-10px radius).
    *   **Panel (b) [Left Half]:** Very soft, subtle blue-tinted background (e.g., #F0F8FF). Label bold **"(b) Query-Aware Spatial-Preserving Visual Routing"** in the top-left corner.
    *   **Panel (a) [Right Half]:** Very soft, subtle gray-tinted background (e.g., #F8F9FA). Label bold **"(a) Multimodal Speculative Memory Decoding"** in the top-left corner.

*   **The Three Parallel Routing Lanes (From M_filt flowing Right):**
    *   **Top Lane (Draft Path):**
        *   **Memory Bias Vector b:** Small box. Callout: *b_j = \sum_i [j \in Unique(S_i)] \cdot c_i \cdot \alpha*.
        *   *Arrow:* M_filt branches UP to Bias b.
        *   **Draft Model (e.g., Qwen3-VL-2B):** Small Blue block (#2563EB). Internally show a circular arrow for autoregressive generation.

    *   **Middle Lane (KV Memory Path):**
        *   **Memory K/V Precompute:** Small sub-box. Callout: *K_mem_raw = strip_rope(K_mem, R)*.

### 5. Visual Style & Hierarchy Checklist (CVPR/NeurIPS standards)
1.  **Color Palette Strictness:** Do not deviate from the semantic colors. Modality=Green, Visual=Teal/Blue, Models=Blues, KV/Cache=Purple, Query/Budget=Orange. Apply subtle, same-hue gradients (e.g., dark blue to slightly lighter blue top-to-bottom) within these blocks.
2.  **Corner Radii:** Set all bounding boxes to 8px border-radius.
3.  **Shadows/Outlines:** NO heavy drop shadows. Use a 2px solid border slightly darker than the fill color for definition (e.g., dark purple border around a light purple KV box).
4.  **Hierarchical Sizing:**
    *   *Target Model* block must be visually 2x larger than the *Draft Model* block to instantly communicate capacity differences.
    *   *Active Context KV Cache* should be physically larger/longer than the *Pruned Visual KV Cache*.
5.  **Arrow Formatting:** 5px thick, dark charcoal gray (#333333). Heads must be solid filled triangles. Ensure text labels run horizontally along or slightly offset above the lines.
6.  **Avoid Crossings:** By positioning M_filt on the left and fanning it vertically *before* pushing right, and keeping Verification on the extreme right, **zero arrow lines will cross** in this layout.
